Why these numbers aren't a verdict on either ecosystem
  • npm often publishes many small packages from one repo. Monorepos count once per package in the main table, which is why the per-repo version is shown too.
  • All @types packages come from one shared repo, so they share its numbers.
  • Download counts include CI and mirrors, and the two registries count them differently, so "top 5,000" means slightly different things.
  • Commits miss reviews, triage, releases and support in both ecosystems.
