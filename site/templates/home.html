Why this exists

Single points of failure have shaped real incidents: left-pad in 2016, event-stream in 2018, colors and faker in 2022, and the xz backdoor in 2024, where an attacker spent about two years earning a lone maintainer's trust. The lesson of those events is that overstretched volunteers need support.

The OpenSSF Criticality Score, OpenSSF Scorecard and the Harvard and Linux Foundation census studies already map critical open source. What's new here is a dated forecast that gets graded in public, and a simulator anyone can use.

What this data can't show

  • Commits leave out reviews, triage, releases, support and paid work done elsewhere.
  • Git can't tell finished software from software nobody is looking after.
  • A package in a shared repo gets that whole repo's numbers.
